## The time of `mwp_auto`

- The service declares its date fields as „Central European Standard Time” with daylight saving and converts the stored wall-clock times to UTC epochs by that rule; statistics and where clauses use the stored times unconverted (`TIME >= TIMESTAMP '2026-11-22 00:00:00'` selects stored times).
- The stored wall-clock time is UTC: an MWP reading, taken at 06:00 UTC by PSG's own description, equals the hourly value stored at 06:00 (246 of 248 point-days at the eight most variable automatic points; where the stored hours 04–08 differ, the stored 06 in 61 point-days against 1), and the loggers' hourly `Air Pressure` of September 2026 correlates with IMGW-PIB's UTC SYNOP hours best at no shift (Białystok 0.953, Wrocław 0.938, Lublin 0.761; Kraków-Balice at +1 h, a logger whose clock deviates). The service's conversion is therefore one hour off in winter and two in summer.
- On a summer-time start day (2026-03-29) the stored 01:00 and 02:00 come back as one epoch; on 2026-10-25 no hour is lost. Stored times are 1 to 6 s past the hour by point.
- In Obserwator (ADR-035 W9): the returned epoch is taken to its Europe/Warsaw wall time, read as UTC and floored to the hour; the pair of a summer-time start day is told apart by a page of the stored hour 02 that the harvest asks for.

## Open questions

1. The day PIG-PIB replaces the month of table 1 (S0's daily runs show it; `store check` reports a month never captured as `not_captured`).
2. The stability of `ID` (R4): `station_identity_changed` names an `ID` whose CBDH number or place changed between versions.
3. The vertical datum of `RZEDNA_TERENU` (R7): not stated, so `H_asl` is „m n.p.m.” without a datum name.
4. The keys `screen_top_m`/`screen_bottom_m` of `hydrogeology` hold `GLEBOKOSC_W_OD`/`GLEBOKOSC_W_DO`, whose aliases read „Głębokość ww. strop/spąg”: the top and bottom of the water-bearing layer, not of a screen (the GUI labels them „Strop/Spąg warstwy wodonośnej”).
5. The hourly rows of `PSH_MONITORING_ID` 0 and of stored days back to 2000-01-02, before the hourly series' first year 2015 (found in S0's backfill): `0` matches no code of the grammar; whether they are test rows is PIG-PIB's to say.
6. Revisions after a month leaves the service (R6): only a request shows them.
